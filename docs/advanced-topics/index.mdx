# Advanced Topics & Misc

These lessons are optional. Each one goes further into something from the main lessons, with heavier
math or a more specialized technique that would slow down a first read. Nothing in the main curriculum
depends on them, and each page says which lesson to read first.

## What's here

- **[Inverse Kinematics: Jacobians & Numerical Solvers](./inverse-kinematics-jacobians.mdx)**: solve arm
  angles a small step at a time, for mechanisms without a tidy geometric answer, and stay stable near
  awkward poses. Builds on [Inverse Kinematics](../path-following/inverse-kinematics.mdx).
- **[How Does Pedro Pathing Work?](./how-pedro-pathing-works.mdx)**: the ideas inside Pedro Pathing's
  follower, from Bézier paths and closest-point search to centripetal correction, heading control, and
  knowing when a path is done. Builds on [Guided Vector Fields](../path-following/guided-vector-fields.mdx).
- **[Take-Back-Half](./takeback-half.mdx)**: a small change to bang-bang that lets a flywheel settle on a
  steady speed. Builds on [Bang-Bang Control](../control-theory/bang-bang.mdx).
- **[RST Pole Placement](./rst-pole-placement.mdx)**: build a velocity controller from your measured
  feedforward constants and the response time you want. Builds on
  [System Identification](../control-theory/system-identification.mdx) and
  [From Math to a Loop](../software-architecture/discretization.mdx).
