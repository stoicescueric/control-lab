# Privacy & Cookies

Control Lab is a static educational website. It does not provide accounts, accept payments, or run an
application backend.

## Essential storage

The site stores your analytics preference in the first-party
`control_lab_analytics_consent` cookie for up to 180 days. Lesson completion and interface preferences
may also be stored locally in your browser. This storage is not used for advertising.

The optional offline mode can cache public site files on your device through a service worker. You can
remove those files through your browser's site-data settings.

## Optional analytics

If you select **Allow analytics**, the site loads Google Analytics 4 using measurement ID
`G-DP9QJ8EJWH`. It records page views and selected interactions with educational demos so the
curriculum can be improved. Advertising storage, ad personalization, and Google Signals are disabled.

No Google Analytics script or request is made before consent. Selecting **Essential only** prevents it
from loading. Changing from allowed to essential-only stops future tracking and removes accessible
Google Analytics cookies from this site; it cannot retract events already sent.

Google processes analytics data under its own terms and privacy policy. Browser extensions or network
controls may block it independently of this setting.

## Embedded videos

YouTube videos use privacy-enhanced `youtube-nocookie.com` embeds and are click-to-load. Opening a
lesson does not contact YouTube; YouTube receives a request only after you choose to load a video.
